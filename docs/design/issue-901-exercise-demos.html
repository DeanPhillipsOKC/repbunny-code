#901 Exercise demos: figure style and placement

A figure drawn by the app from motion data (a real bodyweight squat from the free CMU motion capture set, 5 KB). It lives inside the existing How to section of an exercise's History sheet, so it only plays after a tap and never mid-set. Far-side limbs are dimmer so the near leg reads clearly. Every color comes from the theme, so one data file works in all 7 themes.

Pick: A (Line). It matches the app's thin line icons, reads best at 190px tall, and stays calm next to the steps. B is heavier than anything else on the sheet. C is cute but the ears move with the head and pull the eye from the hips and knees, the part people are checking. If the owner wants the bunny, C could appear only when the bunny is on, like the theme corners do.

A. Line (pick)

Theme accent for the near side and trunk, dim for the far side. How to opens to the figure, then the steps.

B. Solid

Chunky two-tone limbs with a floor shadow. Friendlier from far away, but it gets blobby at the bottom of the squat and covers the joint angles.

C. Bunny ears

Option A with ears on the head. On brand, but the ears move with the head and draw the eye away from the form.

A in Chalk (Just your numbers)

Same data, Chalk tokens. No bunny anywhere, so the line figure fits the plain look too.

A in Sunrise (light)

Light theme check. The dim far side stays visible on white.

Where it's reached from the logger

No new control on the exercise card (its footer line is already full, #492). History, then How to. Two taps, never autoplays during a set.
