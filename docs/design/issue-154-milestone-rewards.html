Issue #154: Milestone rewards in Extras

A new Extras group at the bottom of Account > Preferences > Theme, open to free and Premium users. 13 rewards across 13 milestone tiers (the trial-pass tiers keep their passes). Locked rewards show greyed with their unlock rule; tapping one previews it without picking it. Option A keeps every choice on the Theme screen as tiles. Option B adds five rows that each open a picker, so the Theme screen stays short. The result artboard is the same for both.
