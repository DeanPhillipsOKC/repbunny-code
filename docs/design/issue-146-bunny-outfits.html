#146 Bunny outfits, separate from themes (Premium, 2026-10-06)

A new Outfit group on the Theme screen, right under Mascot. Choices: Match theme (default, today's behavior), Classic, Halloween (witch hat), Autumn, and Holidays, which switches by date: Halloween in October, Autumn in November, Classic the rest of the year (the table lives in src/lib/theme.ts, so a new outfit adds a row). Every card draws its bunny on the current theme's colors, so on Chalk you see the witch hat on graphite.

Theme cards above follow the outfit: with Match theme each card shows its own bunny as today; with any other outfit every card shows that outfit. Minimal hides all art, so the Outfit group dims with a one-line note while it's on (still tappable). Free users preview like themes: the pick applies and the bottom bar names it ("Chalk with the Halloween outfit"). Losing Premium falls back to Classic theme, Match theme.

The question is the picker's shape: A cards three across (bigger art, matches the theme cards), or B rows like Mascot (room for a caption on each, Holidays spells out the schedule).
