#145 Alternate app icons per theme (Premium, 2026-10-05)

A new App icon group on the Theme screen, under Mascot: all 7 icons in a 4-column grid (fits 320px without sideways scroll), the current one ringed in the accent with a check. Tapping one switches the home-screen icon right away. It's its own pick, so the icon doesn't follow the theme: someone can run Chalk inside and keep the Classic icon. Default is Classic. Native apps only; the PWA never shows the group (a web app can't change its icon).

The icons are the real app icon recolored from each theme's tokens by a script (tile = --bg, bunny = --accent, inner ears and face = --text on dark themes or white on light ones, plus a nose color per theme), so new themes get an icon for free. The question is the art: A recolor only, or B recolor plus the theme's own decoration (candy corn, maple leaves, carrots) for the three themes that have one.

Losing Premium keeps the icon (only picking a new one needs it). A Themes trial pass opens the picker too, and the icon also stays after the trial. Free users get the themes-style preview: a home-screen mock with that icon and Keep it with Premium / Go back; nothing changes on the phone until they have Premium.
