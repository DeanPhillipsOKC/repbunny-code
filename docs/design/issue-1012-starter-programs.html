Starter programs: show what's Premium before the tap

Today, the only sign that a program needs Premium is a small sparkle, and a tap on Add is the first time anyone learns why. There's a second trap: free holds 3 routines, so after someone adds one 3-routine program, every other program opens the routine-limit sheet, sparkle or not. Every phone here is drawn in the Easter theme at 375px. No prices and no pop-ups, per docs/decisions.md.

Option A

Free programs first, Premium programs in their own accordion

The free programs keep their equipment groups. The two 5-routine programs move to one closed accordion at the end, whose header states the reason. Fixes: the sparkle surprise. Doesn't fix: the trap after the first add.

Option B

Rows that know your free routine slots

The list layout stays as it is today. A small meter shows how many free routine slots are used, and each row says in words whether that program fits. The program's sheet says what Add will do before anyone taps it. On a fresh install everything shows "Fits." Fixes: the trap after the first add. Pairs with A, which moves the 5-routine programs out of the list.

Option A + B (recommended)

Premium accordion, plus rows that know your free slots

This is A's layout: the free programs in their equipment groups, then the closed Premium accordion. The meter and the per-row fit come from B. Because the 5-routine programs live in the accordion, the rows only ever say Fits or No room, never "Premium". Rows that won't fit still open their description, and the sheet says why before anyone taps Add.

Option C

Pick your equipment first

Three chips at the top filter the list to one equipment group. In each group, the free programs come first, then a divider, then that group's Premium program with a plain label.
