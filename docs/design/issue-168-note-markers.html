#168 Note markers on Template progress

Scope after #158. Story markers (#158) already flag a skipped exercise, planned sets left undone, and swaps on this chart, saved with each workout as it finishes. So the gap left is the workout note. A session with a note gets a marker, and tapping it shows the note in the session card. Older workouts from before #158 have no saved story, and they won't be guessed at by comparing them to today's template, since the template may have changed since then.

Sample: "Push Day", Sep 25 (note only: "Shoulder felt tight, dropped the bench to 155."), and Oct 2 (note plus a skipped exercise).

Option A · Two badges

Pink pencil badge, its own kind

A note gets a pink pencil badge (the user's own words), and a story keeps its lavender bubble. A session with both shows the two badges side by side over the point. In the card, the note comes first as a quote, with the story under it.

Option B · One badge per point (recommended)

One badge, icon says what's inside

Both options · All range, 40 sessions

Dense charts shrink badges to pips

When points sit closer than a badge is wide (under about 18px apart), every marker drops to a small pip over its point, in its badge's color (option B shown). Tapping the point still opens its note and story in the card. Same rule for the existing story badges, which crowd here today.
