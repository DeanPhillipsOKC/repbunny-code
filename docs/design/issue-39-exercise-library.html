Barbell Hip Thrust

Glutes · also works Legs
Equipment
Barbell + bench
Tracked as
Weight × reps
How to▴
  1. Sit with your upper back against a bench, bar across your hips (a pad helps).
  2. Feet flat, about hip-width, knees bent.
  3. Drive through your heels and squeeze your glutes to lift your hips until your body is flat from shoulders to knees.
  4. Pause, then lower with control.

6 · One-time "check your exercises"

Shown once to accounts that already have exercises, after the update. Names that match the library get their groups filled in; tap a chip to change it. Unmatched ones ask. It can be skipped, and anything unconfirmed can be fixed later in Exercises.
