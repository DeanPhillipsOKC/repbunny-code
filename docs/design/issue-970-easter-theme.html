Easter theme (#970)

Two ways to do a secular spring theme from the art: eggs, baskets, carrots, and flowers. Both reuse the carrot for the week tracker and record confetti, so no new decor is needed.

Option A Chosen

Pastel light scheme, lavender lead

A cream page with white cards and lavender tinted panels, like the gingham in the basket. A deep lavender leads, robin’s egg teal is the secondary, butter gold the highlight, carrot orange the errors. The only pastel light theme, and the pink bunny pops on cream the way it does in the art.
