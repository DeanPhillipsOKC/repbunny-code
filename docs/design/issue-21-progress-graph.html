C. "What's est. 1RM?" explainer

What's est. 1RM?

1RM means your one-rep max: the most you could lift for a single rep. You never have to actually try it. RepBunny estimates it from the sets you already log.

  • ⇅Compare any set. 8 reps one week and 5 the next still land on the same scale.
  • ↗See real progress. If the line climbs, you're getting stronger, even when the weight on the bar didn't change.
  • ✓No risky max-out. It's a guide, not a goal. Most accurate for sets of 1 to 12 reps.
