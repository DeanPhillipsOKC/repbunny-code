#20 Backup, restore, and CSV export (plus a new home for plates, #22 follow-up)

Decided with the user 2026-09-29: JSON backup + CSV export, restore replaces everything after a preview and typed confirmation, manual only for now (automatic backups come with Pocket Edition), ships in the current app, always free. The three data actions sit at the top level of the Account sheet. "Plates at my gym" leaves the Account sheet and moves into the plate calculator itself.

1. Account sheet

"Your data" group right under who you're signed in as: back up, restore, CSV. Plates are gone from here. The toast shows what a backup does: the browser saves (or on iPhone, shares) repbunny-backup-2026-09-29.json.
