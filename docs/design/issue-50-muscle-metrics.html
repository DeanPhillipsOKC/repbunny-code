#50 Volume and time per muscle (decisions approved 2026-09-30)

Adds a Sets / Volume / Time switch (dashed outline) to the one-muscle screen from #40. Everything on the screen follows the switch: the big number, the average, the bars, and the "This week" list. The range pills keep their selection. "Also works" muscles get ½ credit on all three. Volume uses the finish recap's rule: bodyweight and assisted sets use your body weight, level and timed sets are left out. A pill only shows if the muscle has that kind of data, and a muscle with sets only gets no switch at all, so it looks exactly like today. Muscle groups list and hub tile don't change.
