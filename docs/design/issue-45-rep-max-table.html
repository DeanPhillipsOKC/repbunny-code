#45 Rep-max table in exercise history (defaults approved 2026-09-29)

A "Rep maxes" card right under the progress chart in the exercise history sheet, Weight exercises only, always visible. Actual = the heaviest weight you've lifted for at least that many reps (so an 8-rep set also counts as your 1RM through 7RM if nothing heavier beats it), with the date and a small "×N" when it came from a bigger set. Est. = what your best est. 1RM predicts for that rep count (Epley, same as the chart). Mixed lb/kg sets are converted to the chart's unit. Plot column (user's idea): each row gets a point for both, so you can see where you're ahead of or behind the estimate without doing the math: solid pink for actual, dashed lavender for est., further right = heavier.
