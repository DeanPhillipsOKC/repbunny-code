#158 Story markers for unusual workouts

A session with a swap, a skipped exercise, or an early finish gets a lavender marker on its chart point. Tapping the point tells the story in plain words. Lavender (--secondary) keeps it apart from the pink line and the gold best dot, and never reads as an error. Exercise charts only show the parts of the story that involve that exercise. The Workout log line (right) is the same in both options.

Sample: template "Leg Day A", Oct 2 session. Leg Press was swapped for Hack Squat (2 sets), Calf Raise was skipped, and the workout ended with 3 planned sets left.

Option A · Template progress

Badge above the point

A small lavender speech-bubble badge floats over each marked point. Tap the point: the normal tooltip shows, and the story lines go in the session card below the chart, where the session's lifts already are.
