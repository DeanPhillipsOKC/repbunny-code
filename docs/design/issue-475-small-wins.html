#475 Small-win chips

A set that's the heaviest yet, a rep best at its weight, an est. 1RM best (Premium) or a target hit gets one chip on its logged row (strongest win only) and a line per win on the rest screen. No takeover; a big record still gets the full-screen celebration and the chip too.

Option A · chip under the set (chosen)

The chip sits on a short line of its own under the values, starting under the Weight column like the target chip on open rows. Long labels and kg both fit. Rows without a win stay their normal height.
