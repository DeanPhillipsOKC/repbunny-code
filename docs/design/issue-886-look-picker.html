Setup step 2: picking a look

Chosen: option A (owner, 2026-10-31, #886), with the link row from issue-886-theme-discovery.html option S1 in place of the line under the tiles.

Today the step offers three looks: Classic with the bunny (the default), Classic, no bunny, and Chalk. These options make it a clearer choice between playful and all business. Each phone works, so tap to try them.

Option A

Two big tiles

Pick your look

Cute or all business. It's your call.

Closest to today. Same tile layout, cut to two, with bigger previews. The no-bunny tile is drawn in Chalk so it reads as its own sharp look, not the leftover option. "Classic, no bunny" moves to Preferences.

Option B

Side by side

Pick your look

Cute or all business. It's your call.

More themes, and the bunny on or off with any of them, in Preferences › Theme.

The most visual. Two tall previews you can compare at a glance, like picking a phone wallpaper. The previews are smaller per look, and the label carries the "fan favorite" nudge instead of a sticker. A sticker fits here too.

Option C

Colors plus a bunny switch

Pick your look

Cute or all business. It's your call.

ColorsClassic pink or Chalk blue
Bunny art and confettiThe bunny cheers you on and celebrates records

More themes in Preferences › Theme.

The clearest about what the bunny is. Colors and the bunny are two separate choices, the way the app already stores them, so all four combinations are one tap away. It's the least like a "pick one" moment, and a switch reads as a setting, which makes the bunny feel optional rather than the brand.

My pick is A. It's the smallest change from today's step: two tiles instead of three, bigger previews, and a no-bunny tile that looks deliberate. The sticker sits on the Playful tile, so the bunny stays the default and the playful look is still the one being shown off. B is the strongest visually, but its previews are half as wide. C is the most flexible, but it's the one most likely to make people turn the bunny off without trying it.

In all three, the bunny stays on by default, and Preferences keeps every combination.
