#137 Training calendar: the last 12 months as a heatmap (Premium, 2026-10-05)

One square per day for the last 53 weeks, Sunday-start columns like the rest of Progress. Shade = sets logged that day in 4 steps (1 to 9, 10 to 19, 20 to 29, 30+), mixed from the accent and surface tokens so light themes work too. Today has an outline. Month labels on top, Mon/Wed/Fri on the left. No streaks, ever.

The grid scrolls sideways on its own, starting at today (right edge); the page never scrolls sideways. Squares are 14px with a 3px gap: 18 weeks show at 375px, 14 at 320px. Tap a day: one workout opens it in the Workout log (expanded and scrolled to); two or more opens the picker. Empty and future days do nothing.
