Valentine's Day theme (#969)

Two ways to keep Valentine's Day apart from Classic, its nearest theme. Both put a rosy heart on the week tracker and drop hearts as record confetti, with gold accents from the art.

Option A Chosen

Burgundy base, hot pink lead

A deep burgundy background, the ribbon color in the art, so it reads red where Classic reads plum and Christmas reads wine. Hot pink buttons and timer bar match the bunny's fur, blush is the secondary, gold the highlight. Records go mint and errors warm orange so neither looks like the pink.
