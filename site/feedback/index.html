Thanks for trying RepBunny. Every message gets read by a real person, and the good ideas end up in the app.

Report a bug

The app crashed, froze, or showed an error.

Tap the person icon at the top right of any screen, then Report a bug under About. Say what you tapped and what happened. The report includes the errors RepBunny logged, so we can find the problem faster. You can read all of it before it goes out.

It opens your email app with the report filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. See how it works.

Request a feature

Something's missing, or could work better.

Tap the person icon, then Request a feature, right under Report a bug. Tell us what you wish RepBunny did and how you'd use it. It goes out through your email app the same way.

Just email us

Anything else: thoughts, questions, what you love or don't.

Write to hello@repbunny.app. A screenshot helps if it's about something on screen.
