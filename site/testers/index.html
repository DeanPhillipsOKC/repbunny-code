Thanks for testing RepBunny

You're one of a small group trying RepBunny on Android before it's in the Play Store for everyone. Here's how to get it and what helps most.

Join the test onGoogle Play

  1. Join the test

    On your Android phone, tap the Google Play button above (or open play.google.com/apps/testing/app.repbunny). Use the Google account your invite was sent to, since only addresses on the tester list can join.

  2. Tap Become a tester

    The page says you're a tester once it's done.

  3. Install RepBunny

    Open RepBunny in the Play Store and tap Install. If the store says the app isn't available, give it a few minutes after joining and try again.

  4. Log your real workouts

    Use it the way you'd use any workout log. Updates arrive through the Play Store like any other app.

What helps most

Anything that's confusing, slow, or broken. Small things count: a button you couldn't find, a number that looked wrong, a screen you didn't understand. "I expected X and got Y" is the most useful kind of note.

Premium is on us

You're set up as a test buyer, so you can unlock Premium for free and no money changes hands. When you tap to buy, Google Play's payment screen should show Test card, always approves as the payment method.

If you see your own card or another real payment method instead, stop and don't buy. Tell us and we'll sort it out first.

Premium stays with your Google account, so if you uninstall and reinstall, tap the person icon, then Restore Purchases, to get it back.

How to tell us

Tap the person icon at the top right of any screen, then Report a bug or Request a feature. It opens your email app with the details filled in, and nothing goes out until you tap Send. See how it works.

Or email hello@repbunny.app. A screenshot helps if it's about something on screen.

Your data stays on your phone

RepBunny has no account and no analytics. What you log is stored on your phone only, so we never see your workouts. A bug report only includes what you choose to send. Privacy policy.
