Made for your gym

Machines, dumbbells, cardio. All in one place.

Most lifting apps assume a barbell and a spreadsheet brain. RepBunny logs the way your gym actually works.

  • Machines with a setting

    That ab machine you roll up on an incline? Log its setting, like level 4. Weight stack machines still log in pounds.

    How load types work →

  • Assisted counts

    Assisted pull-ups and dips get tracked right, so less help shows up as progress.

    How load types work →

  • Cardio too

    Treadmill, bike, stair stepper. Time, distance, incline, and level, right in the same workout.

    Log timed sets →

  • On the clock

    Planks, dead hangs, wall sits, farmer's carries. Tap the stopwatch and go. It chimes at your goal, then puts the time in the set.

    Time a set →

New to lifting?

You don't need a plan. Grab one.

Pick a starter program like Beginner full body, three days a week, and it shows up as routines you can change any way you like. Next time, RepBunny shows what you did last time and what to try today, with the reasoning right there. Take the hint or ignore it.

  • Full body, upper/lower, or push/pull/legs
  • Your last set, ready to repeat in one tap
  • A rest timer with a bunny who waits with you

No streaks. No guilt.

Missed a week? Nothing resets.

Life happens. RepBunny's milestones only add up, so a busy week never costs you anything. With Premium, showing up unlocks new week marks, confetti, sounds, and bunny personalities. On the free plan, milestones earn you trial passes to try Premium.

  • Milestones that stay earned. Workouts, records, and strong weeks add up for good.
  • Nobody's watching. No feed, no leaderboard, no followers. Your numbers are yours.
  • Notice what helps. Tag your sleep, energy, and stress, and see what lines up with your best days.
  • A monthly report card. How the month went next to the one before, plus a recap of your year.

Who's behind RepBunny

It started as a gift.

Christa Bunn Bunn

Christa is the bunny the app is named after. I wanted her to have a way to track her gym progress without paying for an app, and I wanted it to be really cute. Now she lifts with me, tests every build, weighs in on the design, and keeps me honest at the gym.

Dean Deanie Bear

Years of bad habits started catching up with me, so I decided to take my health and fitness more seriously. I've lost over 90 lb so far, and I'm still going. I build RepBunny and log every workout in it.

Why it's an app now. While I was building it, I noticed most workout apps felt like spreadsheets or were made for guys. So the gift turned into an app for anyone who's new to lifting and wants their progress to feel like a win.
