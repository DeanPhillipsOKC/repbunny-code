RepBunny for iPhone and Android

Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • Your workouts stay on your phone.
  • RepBunny collects nothing. We can't see your data, and we never sell it.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
  • Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

What the app stores

RepBunny stores what you enter, such as exercises, sets, reps, weights, times, workout routines, notes, and your settings, in the app's storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your phone's own backup may include the app's data. On iPhone, that's iCloud or computer backup, handled by Apple under your device settings and Apple's privacy policy. On Android, it's the backup to your Google Account, handled by Google under your device settings and Google's privacy policy. We can't see or open either one.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking code, and it does not use your advertising identifier. We don't ask for your name, email address, or location, and there is no account to create.

Backups and exports you make

When you use Back up my data or Export sets as CSV, the app creates a file and hands it to you. You choose where it goes, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or a message to yourself. We never receive a copy. Wherever you keep it, that service's own privacy terms apply.

The app also keeps an automatic copy of your data after every workout, on unless you turn it off. On iPhone it goes to your own iCloud Drive, in the RepBunny folder, or stays in the app's storage when iCloud Drive is off. On Android it goes to the Documents folder on your phone. If you pick a different folder, it goes there instead. The copy goes to your own storage, never to us, and we can't see or open it. iCloud Drive is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and a folder from another app or service follows that service's terms.

Bug reports you send

The app keeps a short log of errors on your device, such as when something failed, which screen you were on, the error message, and the names of the last few screens and buttons you used before it. It's there to describe what went wrong, not what you entered, so it never holds your reps, weights, exercise names, or notes, and it never leaves your device on its own. If you use Report a bug, or tap Send report when the app offers one after an error, you choose which errors go in the report and can add your own description. The report also lists your app version, phone model, and operating system, and you can preview all of it first. The app then opens your email app with the report filled in. Nothing goes out until you tap Send there. We use a report only to fix the problem, the same as any email to us.

Purchases

On iPhone, in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. On Android, they're processed by Google through Google Play. The store tells the app whether a purchase is active so it can unlock Premium. We don't receive your name, email address, or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy or Google's privacy policy for how each store handles purchases.

Emailing us

If you email hello@repbunny.app, we use your address and message only to reply to you. If you check Email me when it's fixed in a bug report, we also email you once when the fix is out. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email.

This website

repbunny.app has no cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and it loads nothing from third-party sites. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which may briefly keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Children

RepBunny is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. We won't start collecting your data without saying so here first.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@repbunny.app.
