Common questions

How do I back up my workouts?

Tap the person icon at the top right, then Back up my data. It saves one file with everything: your workouts, exercises, routines, and settings.

Keep that file somewhere off your phone, like iCloud Drive or an email to yourself, so it's safe if your phone is lost.

How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone, tap Back up my data and save the file to iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it to the new phone.
  2. Install RepBunny on the new phone.
  3. Tap the person icon, then Restore from a backup, and pick the file.

Restoring replaces what's on the new phone with the backup, so do it before you log anything there. Finish any workout in progress first.

How do I restore a purchase?

Premium is tied to your store account, not to one phone. On iPhone, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought it with. On Android, use the same Google account in Google Play. Then open RepBunny and tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen. Premium doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android, since each store handles its own purchases.

Your workout log never needs restoring this way. Logging is always free.

Can I open my sets in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap the person icon, then Export sets as CSV. The file opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets.

A CSV file can't be restored into the app. To keep a copy you can restore, use Back up my data.

Is logging really free?

Yes. Every workout, set, and rep, every exercise type, and your full history are free, with no limits and no trial timer. Backing up, restoring, and exporting are free too. Premium adds extras like the plate calculator, unlimited routines, and reports.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. RepBunny has no account and sends nothing to us. The privacy policy has the details.
