How-to guide

Routines

A routine is a workout you repeat, like Push day. Start it with one tap, and each set starts from what you did last time.

58-second clip, with sound

Make a routine and start it

  1. On Home, tap Build a routine, name it, and tap Next.
  2. Search for each exercise and tap + Add, then Done.
  3. Tap + or − to set how many sets each one gets.
  4. Back on Home, tap the routine to start it.

  1. Make a routine

    Open Routines and tap Add routine. Give it a name and tap Next. The Add exercise list opens: tap each exercise you want, then Done.

    The free App Store version includes 3 routines. Premium removes the limit.

  2. Set it up

    Tap − or + to set how many sets each exercise gets. Drag the dots on the left to change the order, or tap the trash can to remove one (Undo brings it back). Tap the name at the top to rename it. Changes save as you make them.

  3. Pick a category

    RepBunny guesses one from the exercises and marks it Auto. Tap Upper, Lower, Full, Cardio, Core, or Other to choose your own, or Use automatic to go back. It sets the icon on Home.

  4. Start it from Home

    Under What are you training today?, tap the routine. The workout starts right away with a row for each planned set, filled in from last time.

  5. Turn a past workout into one

    Did a Freeform workout you want to repeat? In Progress, open Workout log and tap the workout. Tap Save as routine, name it, and tap Save. The name starts as what you trained, like Upper body push or Full body. If you don't have any routines yet, the recap right after a Freeform workout offers this too.

  6. Done with one?

    In Routines, tap the archive icon on its row, then Confirm archive. It leaves your list and Home, and your past workouts stay in your log. To bring it back, scroll to Archived routines at the bottom of Routines and tap Restore.
