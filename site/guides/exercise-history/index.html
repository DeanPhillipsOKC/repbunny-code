How-to guide

Exercise history

Every exercise keeps its own history: a chart of your progress, a target for next time, and every set you’ve logged. Check it mid-workout to know what to aim for.

45-second clip, with sound

Read a lift’s history

  1. Open Progress and tap Lift progress, then a lift.
  2. Read the chart, and tap Heaviest or another metric to switch it.
  3. Scroll to Next time and Best reps at each weight.
  4. Below them, every session, with a gold paw on each record.

  1. Open it

    During a workout, tap History under the exercise name. You can also open it from Exercises (tap the exercise) or from Progress, under Lift progress.

  2. Read the chart

    After two sessions, a chart shows your trend. Switch what it shows, like Est. 1RM, Heaviest, or Volume, and the time range: 1M, 3M, 1Y, or All. Tap What’s est. 1RM? to see how it’s worked out. Tap a dot to see its math; See the sets › highlights the set below. In the App Store app, Heaviest over 1M or 3M is free; Est. 1RM, Volume, 1Y, and All are part of Premium, and tapping one shows a preview first.

  3. Beat your Next time target

    For weight exercises, Next time gives you a weight and reps to aim for. It adds one rep at your last weight until you reach the top of the 8 to 12 rep range, then says Go up to the next weight. The line under it explains why. With Premium, it also reacts to how hard last time felt, and Why this? shows the math (see Next-set targets).

  4. Go for a rep record

    Best reps at each weight lists the most reps you’ve done at each weight. Last marks the one you used most recently. Beat any row for a rep PR. It’s part of Premium in the App Store app; Next time stays free.

  5. Look back at past sessions

    Under Sessions, each workout is a card with its date, its top set, and your sets lined up in columns. A gold paw marks any set that was a record. Tap any set to see why it was or wasn’t one: the rule, your best before it with both numbers worked out, and what would beat it. You see your last 10 sessions; tap Show older for more.
