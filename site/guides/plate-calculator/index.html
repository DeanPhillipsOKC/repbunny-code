How-to guide

Plate calculator

Skip the mental math. Pick a weight and RepBunny shows which plates go on each side of the bar, using the plates your gym actually has. It’s part of Premium.

42-second clip, with sound

Load the bar

  1. Start a workout with a barbell lift.
  2. Tap Plates. It opens at your set’s weight.
  3. Read Each side, or tap + for the next weight you can load.
  4. Tap Use to put that weight in your set.

  1. Start a barbell lift

    Start a workout and pick a barbell lift, like bench press or squat. A Plates button shows up next to lb and kg. Lifts you made yourself get it once you open them in Exercises and turn on Uses a barbell.

  2. Tap Plates

    It opens at the weight in your set. Type a different total, or tap − and + to step through the weights you can load. The picture and Each side show what goes on, smallest plates outside.

  3. Set your gym’s plates and bar

    Next to Your plates, tap Edit, then tap a plate to turn it on or off so the list matches your gym. Next to Bar, tap Change to pick a women’s, EZ curl, or other bar. The bar is saved for that lift only.

  4. Can’t make it exactly?

    If your plates can’t add up to that weight, you get the closest one lighter and the closest one heavier. The lighter one is picked for you. Tap the other to switch.

  5. Use it

    Tap Use and the weight goes into your set, ready to log.
