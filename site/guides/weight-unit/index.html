How-to guide

Switch between lb and kg

Lift in pounds or kilograms. Set your usual unit once, and change it for a single exercise whenever you need to.

  1. Set your usual unit

    Tap the account button at the top right, then Preferences. Under Weight unit, pick lb or kg. New exercises start in this unit. You also pick it the first time you open the app, on the Which unit do you lift in? screen.

  2. Switch one exercise

    While you log, tap lb or kg on the line above the sets. Weights you have typed but not saved convert for you. Sets you already logged keep the unit they were saved in.

  3. Your history stays right

    Charts and totals convert sets logged in the other unit, so switching never breaks your progress.
