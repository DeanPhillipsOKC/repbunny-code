How-to guide

Monthly reports

On the 1st of each month, Progress gets a report on the month you just finished, side by side with the month before. From January there's a recap of your whole year too. This week's numbers are free; the reports are part of Premium.

45-second clip, with sound

Open last month’s report

  1. Open Progress and scroll to the bottom.
  2. Tap the report tile, for example September report.
  3. Read the totals, your records, your biggest gains, and the muscles you trained most.
  4. Tap the arrow beside the month to look back further.

  1. Check this week

    Open the Progress tab. Under the bunny, a line shows this week's workouts, sets, and records. The week starts on Sunday, same as everywhere else in the app.

  2. Open the report

    Scroll to the bottom of Progress and tap the report tile, for example September report. You'll see workouts, sets, volume, time on timed sets, and records, each with how it compares to the month before. Below that: every record you set, your three biggest est. 1RM gains, and the muscles you trained most.

  3. Look back further

    Use the arrows beside the month to step back through earlier months. Once you've trained in an earlier year, tap Year at the top for the year recap, with the same layout for the whole year.

  4. Share it with your coach

    Tap Share as PDF under the report's title. Pick the range: the month you're looking at, the last 4 weeks, the last 3 months, or Custom for your own first and last day. Tap Share PDF and send it however you like. The PDF lists each lift with its sessions, best set, and est. 1RM at the start and end, then sets per muscle group, your body weight if you've logged it, and your records. Turn on Include the sets behind each number to add a section listing the sets those numbers came from and the set each record beat. It's made on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

  5. Without Premium

    Tapping the tile shows a blurred preview of your report, and Share as PDF lets you look at the ranges but not share. Tap Keep it with Premium to unlock it, or Go back to return to Progress. The This week line stays free.
