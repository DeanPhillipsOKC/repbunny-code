How-to guide

Report a bug

Something went wrong? Send us what happened, along with the errors RepBunny logged, right from the app. You see everything before it goes out.

  1. Open Report a bug

    Tap the person icon at the top right of any screen to open Account. Report a bug is under About.

    If something goes wrong, RepBunny may ask “Send a report?” at the top of the screen. Tap Send report and the sheet opens with that error already checked.

  2. Say what happened and pick the errors

    A sentence about what you tapped and what you saw helps the most. RepBunny lists the errors it logged, and the last 24 hours start checked. Uncheck anything you’d rather not send. Tap Preview report to read the whole thing, including the screens and buttons you used just before the error and your phone model.

    Want to hear back when it’s fixed? Check Email me when it’s fixed. We reply to every report either way.

  3. Send it from your email app

    Tap Email report. Your email app opens with the report filled in and addressed to hello@repbunny.app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send there.

Still stuck?

Email hello@repbunny.app and tell us what happened. A screenshot helps.
