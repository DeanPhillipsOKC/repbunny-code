How-to guide

Restore points

RepBunny quietly saves copies of your data on your phone, so a bad update or restore never costs you your workouts. Restore points are in the iPhone and Android apps.

  1. What gets saved

    The app saves a copy before each update, after every finished workout, and before you restore. It keeps the last 10. Copies stay on your phone, and you don't have to do anything.

  2. Go back to one

    Tap the account button at the top right, then Your data, then Restore points. Copies are listed newest first, with the date and how many workouts each holds. Tap one to see what's in it. Nothing changes until you confirm by typing RESTORE, the same as restoring from a file. Anything you logged after that copy won't be in it.

    If you're mid-workout, the row says Finish your workout first. Finish or discard it, then try again.

  3. If RepBunny can't open your workouts

    After a failed update, RepBunny shows This update couldn't finish. Your workouts are safe and unchanged. Update again when the fix reaches the store. If your data is damaged, it shows Something went wrong opening your workouts and offers the newest restore point. Tap it to pick up from there, or tap See all restore points to choose another. The damaged copy is kept aside, not deleted.

  4. Also save a file copy

    Restore points live on your phone, so losing or resetting the phone loses them. For a copy that survives that, save a file from Your data and keep it somewhere safe.
