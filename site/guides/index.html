Guides

Short, step-by-step help with screenshots for RepBunny’s features.

Logging

  • Lifting basics New to this? A 90-second video on sets, reps, picking a weight, rest, RPE, and gym words.
  • Log a workout Start, log your sets, add exercises, and finish.
  • Rest timer Time your rest between sets, even when you leave the screen.
  • Plate calculator See which plates go on each side of the bar.
  • Load types Weight, level, bodyweight, assisted, or timed.
  • Merge duplicate exercises Combine two copies of a lift into one and keep every set.
  • Next-set targets What to aim for today, based on how last time felt.
  • Swap a taken machine Trade an exercise for a close match, just for today.
  • Fix a past workout Change, add, or delete sets, fix the date, or log a workout you missed.

Routines & programs

  • Routines Save a workout you repeat and start it in one tap.
  • Starter programs Add a proven plan as routines you can change.

Progress

  • Exercise history Your progress chart and what to aim for next time.
  • Muscle groups and balance Sets per muscle, and whether push and pull are even.
  • Training calendar A year of training at a glance, one square per day.
  • Monthly reports How your month went, a recap of your year, and a PDF for your coach.
  • Strength levels Where your big lifts stand for your body weight.
  • Body weight Log a weigh-in, read your trend, and fix a mistake.
  • Body measurements How to measure, and how to track your waist, arms, and more.
  • How did it feel? Tag sleep, energy, and stress, and see what helps you lift.
  • Milestones What counts, what they unlock, and how to use a trial pass.

Make it yours

  • Switch between lb and kg Set your weight unit, or change it for one exercise.
  • Themes Give the app a new look, or tone the bunny down.
  • Extras Week marks, confetti, sounds, and bunny personalities your milestones unlock.
  • Home screen widgets Your week, your next workout, and your latest record, without opening the app.

Your data & help

  • Bring your workouts from Strong or Hevy Import your past workouts so records and charts work from day one.
  • Back up just what you need Save only your routines, preferences, or history.
  • Restore points Automatic copies you can go back to after a bad update or restore.
  • Report a bug Send us what went wrong, with the errors the app logged.
