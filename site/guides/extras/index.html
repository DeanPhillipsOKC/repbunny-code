How-to guide

Extras

Your milestones unlock little extras: new marks for your week tracker, confetti for your records, rest chimes, record fanfares, and new personalities for the bunny. They're part of Premium, and you earn them by training: each milestone opens one up. Milestones you reached before getting Premium count too, so their extras unlock the moment you get it.

  1. Open Extras

    Tap the account button at the top right, then Preferences, then Theme, and scroll down to Extras. Each row shows what you're using now and how many you've unlocked. A New tag means a milestone just unlocked something there. (When a workout unlocks one, you can also tap Use it now on the screen that shows up after you finish.)

  2. Pick one

    Tap a row, then tap what you want. It applies right away. Sounds play when you tap them, so you can hear them first. Match theme brings back your theme's own week mark or confetti, like Halloween's candy corn.

  3. Peek at a locked one

    Locked extras are greyed out and say what unlocks them, like 25 workouts. Tap one to see a preview and how close you are. You can't pick it yet, but now you know what you're working toward. Without Premium, every extra but the default is tagged Premium. You can still tap one to see it or hear it, and Keep it with Premium shows what Premium includes.

  4. See them in action

    Your week mark shows on Home's This week tracker. Confetti and the fanfare play when you set a record. The chime plays when your rest timer ends. The chimes and fanfares are short recordings, like a boxing bell or an air horn, and they play offline too. The bunny talks on the rest timer and at the top of your workout recap. For everyone, it says what you just did, like "2 more reps than last time on Leg Press" or how many workouts you've done this week. Your bunny personality sets how it says it: Classic keeps it plain, Punny adds bunny puns, Coach keeps it short like a cue, and Hype cheers you on. To see every milestone and what it unlocks, go to Progress, then Milestones.
