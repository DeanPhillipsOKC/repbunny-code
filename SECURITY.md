# Security policy

Security research and privacy checks are welcome. RepBunny's promise is that nothing you log leaves your phone in the native apps, and we'd like people to check it.

## Report a problem

- **Security problems:** use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository (the "Report a vulnerability" button under the Security tab).
- **Anything else** (bugs, questions, privacy concerns): email hello@repbunny.app. Issues are turned off on this repository.

Please don't post details of a security problem publicly before we've had a chance to fix it.

## In scope

- The app (the installable web app and the Android and iPhone builds).
- How it handles your data, including any path where logged data could leave the device.
- The build and release workflows in `.github/`.

## Good-faith research

If you act in good faith (no access to other people's data, no service disruption, no spam), we won't pursue you for it. Tell us what you found and give us a reasonable time to fix it.
